# Introduction to AI agents

## Definitions

An **agent** is anything that can be viewed as perceiving its environment through **sensors** and
acting upon that environment through **actuators**

A **reasoning LLM** is capable of thinking and generating thoughts through autoregresive behavior.

**Context engineering** is an attempt to balance information available and what we give to the LLM.

**Planning** is breaking down a large task into smaller, actionable steps, referred to as task decomposition.

Planning and reflection create an interactive loop of planning out tasks, taking actions, and reflecting on the output.
